Breville / Sage Oracle Error rror 24: boiler water level or pump fault

Error rror 24 bedeutet: Coffee boiler water level probe fault.

Kurzantwort. The boiler water-level probe is not reporting what the controller expects: the boiler should be filling and the level probes should register water, and they do not. Scaled probes, an inlet line blocked with scale, a small fill pump that has stopped moving water, or a probe seal leak all produce this code.
Betroffenes Bauteilboiler water-level probe
Schweremedium
Selbstreparaturmoderate
Wann der Fehler auftrittjederzeit

Error rror 24 beheben

  1. Tank full and seated, then power cycle and let the machine attempt a fill.
  2. Descale, including the steam boiler cycle if your model has one. Scale on the level probe is insulating and reads as "no water".
  3. Use the service mode Live Debug screen to watch the boiler level reading while the pump runs.
  4. Open the machine and clean the probes with a scale remover, replace their o-rings, and check the inlet hose for blockage.
  5. If the fill pump buzzes and no water reaches the boiler, replace the small pump. Some owners top the boiler up with a syringe through the probe port to get the machine running while parts are on order.

Wann aufhören und zum Reparateur gehen

Water and mains electricity share the top of the boiler. Unplug before touching the probes.

Was es kostet

Ersatzteile: Level probe and o-ring kit about $85; steam boiler fill pump $30 to $60.

Service: Der Herstellerservice für einen Vollautomaten liegt außerhalb der Garantie typisch bei 250–500 € inkl. Rückversand; freie Espressowerkstätten sind bei einem einzelnen Teil meist günstiger.

Reparatur lohnend? Yes. Probes, o-rings and a fill pump are all under $100 and this is the single most common Breville dual-boiler repair.

Fehlerprotokoll abrufen

Leave the machine plugged in but switched off. Press and hold 1 CUP, 2 CUP and POWER together for at least one second. After the long beep the LCD shows the firmware version (starts with "Fr"). Press the SELECT dial once to open Error Storage, then rotate it to step through errors 1 to 32 and the stored count for each. Scroll past error 32 to "ErSt" and hold SELECT for 3 seconds to clear the log.

Löschen Sie das Protokoll nach der Reparatur, um zu prüfen, ob der Defekt wirklich weg ist.
