Breville / Sage Barista Touch Impress ER18: electrical safety protection triggered

ER18 bedeutet: E-fast protection triggered due to a detected electrical or safety fault.

Kurzantwort. The machine detected an electrical fault: leakage current to ground, an unexpected mains waveform, or a heater drawing power when it should not. Water on a heating element or the power board is the everyday cause. This is the code that also trips the GFCI / RCD in your kitchen.

Wann der Fehler auftrittjederzeit

ER18 beheben

  1. Unplug. Do not keep resetting the breaker; each trip is the machine telling you current is going where it should not.
  2. Let the machine dry for 24 hours somewhere warm. A machine that has been descaled or has a slow leak often has water around the element terminals.
  3. Check the mains cable and plug for damage and try a different socket on a different circuit.
  4. If it runs after drying, find and fix the leak (o-rings, hoses, boiler fittings) or it will return.
  5. If it trips again dry, the element insulation or the power board has failed. That is a technician job.

Wann aufhören und zum Reparateur gehen

This is a mains-electricity fault. Unless you are competent with live testing, hand it to a repairer.

Was es kostet

Ersatzteile: Triac / power board $80 to $150; heating element $60 to $150.

Service: Der Herstellerservice für einen Vollautomaten liegt außerhalb der Garantie typisch bei 250–500 € inkl. Rückversand; freie Espressowerkstätten sind bei einem einzelnen Teil meist günstiger.

Reparatur lohnend? Drying out: yes. Element or board on an old machine: get a quote first.

Fehlerprotokoll abrufen

Unplug the machine. Press and hold the Power button while plugging it back in. When the password screen appears, enter 02015 to open Service Mode, then open the error log.

Löschen Sie das Protokoll nach der Reparatur, um zu prüfen, ob der Defekt wirklich weg ist.
