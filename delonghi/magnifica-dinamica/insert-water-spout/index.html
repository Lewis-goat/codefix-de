De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica Insert Water Spout: spout not detected

Insert Water Spout bedeutet: Machine does not detect the hot-water / milk spout when it needs to run a rinse or relieve pressure.

Kurzantwort. The machine wants the spout on (to rinse, descale, or relieve pressure) and its sensor cannot see it. The spout is not pushed fully on, its O-rings are worn so it sits loose, or the sensor magnet/contact is dirty.
Betroffenes Bauteilspout connection and its sensor

Selbstreparatureinfach
Wann der Fehler auftrittjederzeit

Insert Water Spout beheben

  1. Push the spout on fully; it should click or seat with resistance.
  2. Check the O-rings on the spout stub; replace if flattened.
  3. Clean the spout and the socket; scale here is common.
  4. If the message appears with "Ground too fine", descale the machine.

Wann aufhören und zum Reparateur gehen

Nothing dangerous here.

Ersatzteile: O-ring kit $5 to $10; spout $20 to $40.

Reparatur lohnend? Yes.
